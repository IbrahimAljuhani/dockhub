# ⚙️ Automation

Workflow automation — connecting services together so things happen without you.

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| ✅ [**n8n**](n8n/) | A visual workflow builder with hundreds of integrations. Trigger on a webhook, a schedule or an event, then chain steps across your other services. Self-hosted, so your credentials and data stay on your server. |

---

## 📌 Why this category has one service

It used to hold OpenClaw and Hermes as well. Both turned out to be **AI agent** products rather than workflow automation, so they moved to [AI-Agents](../AI-Agents/) where people would actually look for them.

The distinction is worth keeping: n8n runs **workflows you defined** — deterministic, repeatable, the same every time. An agent decides what to do. Those are different tools for different problems, even when the problem sounds similar.

n8n also has AI nodes, so it can call a model from [AI](../AI/) as one step in a workflow — without handing over control of what happens next.

---

## 📌 Worth knowing

**n8n is a natural home for future additions here** — Node-RED, Windmill and similar tools would sit alongside it.
